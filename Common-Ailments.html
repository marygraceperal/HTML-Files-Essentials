Food Poisoning
Understanding the Illness
  • Type of digestive illness
  • Primarily a digestive tract infection
Key Indicators
  • Symptoms like severe diarrhea
  • Acute stomach and abdominal pain
  • Can also cause fever and dehydration
How to Prevent It
  • Practice meticulous food and hand hygiene
  • Ensure thorough and proper cooking
