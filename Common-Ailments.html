Common Ailments

Introduction

This presentation discusses common ailments and how to prevent them.

Appendicitis
  • Inflammation of the appendix
  • Pain in lower right abdomen
  • Causes: infection, blockage
  • Prevention: water, fiber, hygiene
Food Poisoning
  • Digestive infection
  • Caused by contaminated food
  • Symptoms: diarrhea, stomach pain
  • Prevention: clean food, cook properly
Anorexia
  • Eating disorder
  • Fear of gaining weight
  • Symptoms: weight loss, body image issues
  • Treatment: therapy, medical help
