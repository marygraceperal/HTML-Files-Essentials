Proof should be honest.

GraceFlow Tech should show evidence clearly, distinguish documented results from expectations, and avoid claims that cannot be supported.

01

Show the work

Whenever possible, demonstrate the actual system, artifact, workflow, or output.

02

Document the process

Explain how an outcome was reached rather than presenting only the final result.

03

Separate fact from expectation

Clearly distinguish documented outcomes, observations, plans, and future goals.

04

Keep improving

Proof is also a record of learning, iteration, and continuous improvement.
