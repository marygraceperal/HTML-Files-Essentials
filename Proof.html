What guides the method.

Technology is only useful when it supports a meaningful human or organizational need.

01

People before technology

Understand the people and their context before selecting a technical approach.

02

Purpose before complexity

Build what is necessary to solve the actual problem rather than adding complexity for its own sake.

03

Evidence before assumptions

Use feedback, testing, information, and observable results to guide improvement.

04

Improvement never stops

Digital systems should be able to evolve as needs and circumstances change.
