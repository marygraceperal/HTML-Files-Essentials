Grade 12 Math: Measurement Activities

🗓️ Monday Launch

Activity: "Spot the Mess" Hunt

Task: Review the NASA disaster story and analyze real-world statements with wrong units.

❓ Discussion & Practice:

  1. Why is agreeing on units critical in real life?
  2. Baby's weight is 3,000 liters.
    👉 Correction: Change to Grams or Kilograms!
  3. Door height is 2.5 grams.
    👉 Correction: Change to Meters!
🗓️ Tuesday Session 1

Activity: Sorting & Absurdity Check

Task: Sort objects by scale (Ant=Milli, Finger=Centi, Rice=Kilo) and catch mismatched units.

❓ Discussion & Practice:

  1. Why not measure our classroom length in millimeters?
  2. Drank 500 kilograms of water.
    👉 Correction: Change to Milliliters!
  3. Notebook width is 21 kilometers.
    👉 Correction: Change to Centimeters!
🗓️ Session 2

Activity: Staircase Climb

Task: Find the glitch in a classmate's conversion, then practice same-system steps.

❓ Discussion & Practice:

  1. Big unit to small unit: does the number get bigger or smaller? Why?
  2. Practice: Convert 3,500g to kg.
    👉 Answer: 3.5 kg (Up 3 steps = divide by 1,000).
🗓️ Session 3

Activity: Train Tracks

Task: Use bridge formulas and train tracks to cross between different measurement systems.

❓ Discussion & Practice:

  1. Why can't we use the staircase for inches to centimeters?
  2. Practice: Convert 50 lbs to kg ($1 \text{ lb} \approx 0.45 \text{ kg}$).
    👉 Answer: 22.5 kg ($50 \times 0.45$).
🗓️ Session 4

Activity: Task Force Scenarios

Task: Use the 4-step checklist in teams to solve practical everyday problems.

❓ Discussion & Practice:

  1. What are the 4 steps of our word problem checklist?
  2. Task: Recipe needs 1,500g of flour; scale reads kg. How many kg?
    👉 Answer: 1.5 kg ($1,500 \div 1,000$).
