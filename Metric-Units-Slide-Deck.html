🗓️ Monday • Reading Activity

The $125 Million NASA Climate Orbiter Disaster

The Story: In 1999, NASA lost the $125 million Mars Climate Orbiter because two different teams used mismatched measurement units.

Lockheed Martin calculated thruster performance using Imperial units (pound-seconds), while the NASA team back in mission control processed the data assuming Metric units (Newton-seconds).

Because nobody caught the unit discrepancy, the spacecraft dipped too close to the Martian atmosphere and disintegrated!
