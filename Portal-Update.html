🚧 What Should Be Developed Before Pilot Testing?

The remaining work focuses on organizing approved records, refining access, and making the portal ready for actual school use.

1

Finalize Portal Structure

Finalize the purpose and navigation of Submit a Document, Teacher Document Repository, Approved Documents, and School Programs & Work Areas.

2

Finalize Privacy & Access Control

Define who can view teacher documents, school-program documents, approved records, and assigned work-area records.

3

Develop the Teacher Document Repository

Transform the current Requirements section into an organized repository for Curriculum & Instruction, Assessment, and Teacher Records.

4

Add the Approved Documents Card

Provide the School Head and Master Teacher with a convenient view of approved documents and a secure View File function.

5

Activate School Programs & Work Areas

Connect ARAL, SBM & Governance, School Planning, LAC & Professional Development, School Programs, and Reports & Accomplishments to actual document records.

6

Standardize Drive Organization

Ensure teacher folders follow a consistent naming structure and submitted files are stored in their correct locations.

7

Complete User & Assignment Preparation

Verify teacher accounts, active status, and relevant assignments needed for correct access during the pilot.

8

Conduct End-to-End Testing

Test the complete workflow using Teacher, Coordinator, Master Teacher, and School Head scenarios before pilot use.
