Standard Operating Procedure

📋 Student Self-Management & Observation Plan

A Monthly Rotation Framework for 30 Students

🎯 Target-Driven

👀 Unannounced Observations

🌱 Monthly Growth Cycles

Core Strategy

How The Rotation Plan Works 🔄

1 Target Behavior / Month

Each student selects 1 specific indicator to work on independently for 30 days.

10 Students / Week

The teacher observes 10 students weekly without announcing who is being evaluated.

Week 3 Feedback

Checklists are returned for reflection, self-assessment, and strategy planning.

Phase 1

Monthly Goal Setting (Week 0) 🎯

Empowering Student Choice & Autonomy

  • Student Choice: All 30 students select 1 target habit from the Academic-Life Harmony checklist.
  • Master Logging: Teacher records each student's focus indicator in the master tracking ledger.
  • Personal Goal: Students commit to practicing their target behavior during daily routines.
Phase 2

Observation Rotation (Weeks 1–3) 🕵️‍♂️

Gathering Authentic Classroom Evidence

📅 Cohort Schedule

  • Week 1: Observe Cohort A (Students 1–10)
  • Week 2: Observe Cohort B (Students 11–20)
  • Week 3: Observe Cohort C (Students 21–30)

📝 Observation Rules

  • Blinded Rating: Observations are unannounced to prevent anxiety.
  • Concrete Evidence: Teacher writes specific behavioral notes per student.
Phase 3

Feedback & Reflection (End of Week 3) 💬

Turning Ratings into Actionable Growth

  • Form Distribution: Observation sheets are returned to all 30 students at the end of Week 3.
  • Self-Reflection: Students compare teacher ratings with their internal perception of their progress.
  • Support Consultations: Quick 1-on-1 check-ins for students needing strategy adjustments.
Phase 4

Progression & Next Cycle 🚀

Mastering Habits Step-by-Step

Rating of 3 (Consistently Observed)

The student completes the behavior and selects a NEW behavior for the following month.

Rating of 1 or 2 (In Progress)

The student continues with the SAME behavior for one more cycle to achieve mastery.

Summary

🌟 Building Sustainable Classroom Habits!

Consistency over intensity yields long-term success.
