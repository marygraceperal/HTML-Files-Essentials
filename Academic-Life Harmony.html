The Big Idea

Harmony > Perfection 🌈

❌ What It Is NOT

  • A perfect 50/50 split every day
  • Giving up or doing less work
  • Feeling guilty for taking a rest

✅ What IT IS

  • Studying smarter in school
  • Protecting sleep and health
  • Making room for what you love
Habit #1

Pace Your Pace! ⏳

Time Management & Consistency

  • Slice Big Tasks: Break multi-week projects into daily bite-sized steps.
  • The 20-Minute Rule: 20 minutes of daily review beats 4 hours of late-night cramming.
  • Track Deadlines: Use a planner or checklist so nothing surprises you.
Habit #2

Set Friendly Boundaries 🚧

Respecting Your Workload Capacity

  • Know Your Limits: It's okay to say "I can help tomorrow, but tonight I'm resting."
  • Night-Time Cutoffs: Turn off group project chats after 8:00 PM.
  • Do Your Fair Share: Focus on your assigned role without taking over others' tasks.
Habit #3

Unplug & Recharge 🔌

Intentional Mental Rest

❌ Screen Scrolling

Keeps your brain overstimulated and causes eye strain during breaks.

✅ True Recharge

Stretching, taking deep breaths, drinking water, and stepping away from screens.

Habit #4

Speak Up Early 🙋

Proactive Stress Management

  • Ask Immediately: Don't wait until exam night to ask for help when stuck.
  • Mistakes = Learning: Errors are just data showing you where to practice next.
  • Take One Step: When feeling overwhelmed, pause and do just the very first step.
Habit #5

Fuel Your Brain 🔋

Energy & Self-Care

😴 Sleep (7-8 hrs)

A rested brain retains 2x more information than a tired one!

🥗 Meals & Water

Don't skip lunch—your brain consumes 20% of your body's energy.

🎨 Hobbies & Fun

Keep time for creative activities, sports, family, and play.
